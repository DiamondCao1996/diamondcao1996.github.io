About

Understanding how we work
with emerging technologies.

I am a doctoral student at Chalmers University of Technology, researching cognitive augmentation in production systems. I study how virtual reality, social robots and digital twins can support people in manufacturing.

My work combines human-centered technology evaluation, study design and interaction prototyping, with attention to mental workload, spatial cognition and task performance. My broader interests include affective computing and exploratory applications of AI in health management.
